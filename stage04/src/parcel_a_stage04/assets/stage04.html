How much do the five models differ?

Inter-model CV is the population standard deviation divided by the mean within each native unit, then averaged over area where it is defined. It describes these five model outputs; other uncertainty sources need separate assessment.

How to interpret the results

“Mapped area” means the area with data from all five models. Suitability and yield can cover different parts of the AOI. Valid zero-yield values are retained; missing data are excluded. Positive-yield area uses a five-model mean greater than zero, whichever map view is selected.

GAEZ attainable yield describes the best occurring suitability class within a source cell, in kg dry weight/ha. It is a modelled potential under the source assumptions. The approximately 10 km native grid cannot resolve local field conditions. Each time bar represents a 20-year scenario period; climate pathways are possible futures.
