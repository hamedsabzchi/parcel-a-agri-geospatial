Interpretation and limitations

Suitability consensus is a project-derived comparison of five individual GCMs. Modal agreement is not certainty or probability. GAEZ attainable yield describes the best occurring suitability class in a source cell, in kg dry weight/ha; it is not observed parcel yield. These are 20-year scenario periods, not annual forecasts. SSPs are pathways, not predictions.

Approximately 10 km native data provide context and no field validation. Common valid area is the denominator for comparable five-model percentages. Valid zero and NoData are different. Uncovered areas are not estimated. Positive- and zero-yield areas always use the five-model mean, regardless of the displayed diagnostic.
